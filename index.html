QUESTIONS

Before you send it.

Do you do custom designs?
Yes. Send the idea, artwork, size, colour and where it is going. We can clean it up into a cut-friendly design.
Can I order windshield banners?
Yes. Measure the glass, tell us the exact text, style and colour. We can quote plain, layered, or holographic accent versions.
Can you make layered multi-colour vinyl?
Yes. For clean production we simplify the artwork into separate layers with enough thickness to weed and line up properly.
What files are best?
SVG, AI, EPS or PDF are best. PNGs and screenshots can work if they are high resolution and the design is not too tiny.
